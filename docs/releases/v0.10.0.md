## What's New in v0.10.0

- **Recent Activity Timeline**: A dedicated `最近使用` workspace replaces the old recent-asset grid. Every open, reveal, and copy-path action is recorded locally and grouped by resource.
- **Folded Cards with Details**: The same resource appears only once, with its latest action time and per-action counts visible at a glance. Click to expand the full action history.
- **Time-Range and Action Filters**: Filter activity by `全部`, `今天`, or `最近 7 天`, and by action type (`打开文件`, `打开所在目录`, `复制路径`).
- **Paginated Load-More**: Activity loads in pages of 40; click `加载更多` to append older entries without losing the current view.
- **Missing Asset History**: Activities for missing files remain visible, with open actions disabled.
- **Non-Blocking Activity Recording**: File actions report success independently of activity writes. A recording failure is logged but never reported as an open/reveal/copy failure.
- **Retention and Cleanup**: Activity history is retained for 30 days and capped at the most recent 1,000 records. Cleanup runs automatically at startup.
